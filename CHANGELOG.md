# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First release.

- **Delete permanently** in an archived session's ⋯ menu.
- **Settings → Archived sessions** page: archived sessions grouped by workspace, with restore and delete per session, per workspace, or all at once.
- **Auto-delete** archived sessions after 1, 3, 7, 14, 30, 60, or 90 days (off by default).
- Deleting a workspace archives its sessions; **Restore workspace** brings the workspace and all its sessions back.
- A confirmation before every permanent delete.
